How the automation works

1. AI Qualifier reads a company or job post, picks the best product (Reclu, Yolii, Retrain, Giobs), scores fit and adds the lead.
2. AI email writer drafts a personalised email from the lead's hiring pain and product.
3. Click Mark as sent: the lead moves to Emailed, a follow-up is scheduled for 3 days later and the activity is logged.
4. Status changes set the next step automatically (Replied → book demo in 1 day, Demo booked → send prep, Not interested → re-engage in 90 days).
5. Overdue follow-ups surface here in Today's actions, so no lead is forgotten.
